FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide CE testing in India?

ITC India supports manufacturers with testing associated with CE compliance requirements. The exact tests depend on the product, intended use and applicable EU legislation or standards.

Can you provide a CE certificate?

The CE conformity route depends on the product and applicable European legislation. Some products may follow a manufacturer conformity assessment route, while others require involvement of an appropriate third-party conformity assessment body. Our team can help you understand the testing and compliance requirements applicable to your product.

How do I know which CE standard applies to my product?

Send us your product specifications, intended application and target market. Our technical team can review the requirement and help identify the relevant testing requirements.

Do you provide vibration testing?

Yes. ITC India provides vibration and shock testing for relevant products and components, including random vibration, sine vibration and mechanical shock testing.

Do you provide ATEX testing?

We provide ATEX-related testing and compliance support for products intended for potentially explosive environments. Requirements vary depending on the equipment category, hazardous zone and applicable conformity assessment route.

Do you provide RoHS testing?

Yes. RoHS testing can be used to evaluate restricted substances in applicable electrical and electronic products, components and materials.

What information should I send for a quotation?

For a faster technical review, send:

  • Product name
  • Product specification or datasheet
  • Testing standard, if known
  • Target country or market
  • Number of models
  • Required timeline
  • Any buyer or regulatory testing requirement
